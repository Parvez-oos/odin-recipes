Description

A burger is a popular sandwich made with a cooked meat patty placed inside a sliced bun. It is usually served with lettuce, tomato, cheese, and different sauces.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Ground beef
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Divide ground beef into patties.
  2. Season with salt and black pepper.
  3. Cook the patties thoroughly.
  4. Add cheddar cheese on top.
  5. Toast the burger buns.
  6. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup.
  7. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, and beef patty.
  8. Close the bun and serve.
