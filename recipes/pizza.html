Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a round dough base, tomato sauce, cheese, and different toppings. It is baked until the crust becomes crispy and the cheese melts.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Bell pepper
  • Onion
  • Mushrooms
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough.
  3. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  4. Add mozzarella cheese.
  5. Add your favorite toppings.
  6. Drizzle a little olive oil.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes.
  8. Slice and serve hot.
